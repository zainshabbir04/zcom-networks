FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about Z COM

Clear, transparent answers regarding our network, coverage, and enterprise connectivity.

Which areas in Lahore do you cover?+
Z COM Networks provides fiber-optic coverage across major residential and commercial sectors in Lahore, including Gulberg, DHA, Cantt, Model Town, Johar Town, Faisal Town, Garden Town, and commercial enterprise districts. You can submit your location via our coverage tool or contact us for a free site feasibility survey.
What does Unlimited Data with “No FUP” mean?+
No Fair Usage Policy (No FUP) means your internet connection will never be throttled or slowed down, regardless of how many gigabytes or terabytes you download or upload during the month. You enjoy 100% of your subscribed speed 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
What is Dedicated Internet Access (DIA)?+
Dedicated Internet Access provides an exclusive, non-shared 1:1 symmetrical fiber pipeline directly into your premises. Unlike shared broadband, your bandwidth is entirely reserved for your organization, backed by a strict 99.9% uptime SLA, proactive 24/7 NOC monitoring, and low-latency global transit routes.
What are your customer support hours?+
Our customer support and Network Operations Center (NOC) operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. You can reach our Lahore-based dispatch desk by phone at +92 42 345 11 111, via WhatsApp at +92 317 1118042, or via email at sales@zcomnetworks.com.pk.
How do I get connected or schedule a site survey?+
Simply fill out our Availability Form on the Contact page or send us a message on WhatsApp with your exact location. A technical team member will confirm optical fiber feasibility and schedule a free installation at your convenience.
